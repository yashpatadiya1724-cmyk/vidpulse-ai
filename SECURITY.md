- **Lead Maintainer:** Yash Patadiya
- **Security Contact:** [yashpatadiya1724@gmail.com](mailto:yashpatadiya1724@gmail.com)
- **Response SLA:** Within 48 hours with an initial severity triage and remediation plan.
